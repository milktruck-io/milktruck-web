## Basic Information

## Styling, Feeling, Vibe, Theming
- In addition to the design system, there should be a more structured set of instructions for the general feel, application, and true experience of the site. As it stands now, the example UI of the site in the design system is really not matching my intention with the site. Now, it is pretty race-y, fast, in a way that I am not a huge fan of. It should be homely, almost cozy, welcoming, not as abrasive as the racing feel gives it now. It shouldnt give off like a tech company website, it needs to have a neaighborhoodly kindness to it. It should reflect that in various aspects.
